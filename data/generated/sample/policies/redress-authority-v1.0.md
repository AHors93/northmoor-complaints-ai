# Redress Authority Limits

Version 1.0. Effective from 1 May 2024. Restricted to team leads.

## 1. Limits

- Case handlers: up to £250.00 per complaint.
- Team leads: up to £1,000.00 per complaint.
- Head of complaints: above £1,000.00.

## 2. Breaches

Do not split redress to stay under a limit. Report any payment above authority to the head of complaints.
