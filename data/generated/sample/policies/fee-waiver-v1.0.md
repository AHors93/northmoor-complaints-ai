# Fee Waiver Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 1 April 2023.

## 1. Missed payment fee

A fee of £25.00 is charged when a monthly payment is not received within five days of its due date.

## 2. Fees charged in error

Refund in full any fee charged in error, including a second fee for the same missed payment.

## 3. Goodwill refunds

Goodwill refunds are not available. A fee that was charged correctly must stand.
