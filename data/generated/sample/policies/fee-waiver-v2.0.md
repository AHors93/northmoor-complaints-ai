# Fee Waiver Policy

Version 2.0. Effective from 1 July 2025. Replaces version 1.0.

## 1. Missed payment fee

A fee of £12.00 is charged when a monthly payment is not received within five days of its due date. Fees charged before 1 July 2025 were £25.00.

## 2. Fees charged in error

Refund in full any fee charged in error, including a second fee for the same missed payment. Uphold the complaint.

## 3. Goodwill refunds

If the customer asks and no other missed payment fee was charged in the previous 12 months, refund the fee in full as a gesture of goodwill. The fee was charged correctly, so partially uphold the complaint.

## 4. Repeat fees

Do not refund a correctly charged fee if another was charged in the previous 12 months. Do not uphold the complaint.
