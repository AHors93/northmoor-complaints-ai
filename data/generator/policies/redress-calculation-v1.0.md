# Redress Calculation Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 1 May 2024.

## 1. Financial loss

Put the customer back in the position they would have been in. Refund incorrect fees in full. Refund interest where lending was unaffordable.

## 2. Distress and inconvenience

- Minor inconvenience, such as a long call wait or too many contacts: £50.00.
- Material distress, such as a wrongly issued default notice, misdirected letters or unclear commission disclosure: £150.00.

## 3. Payment

Pay redress by bank transfer within 28 days. Do not set it against arrears without the customer's consent.
