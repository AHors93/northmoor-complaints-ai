# Arrears Handling Policy

Version 2.0. Effective from 3 March 2025. Replaces version 1.0.

## 1. Contact limits

Contact a customer in arrears no more than three times in any seven days, across all channels. Exceeding the limit is a service failing even when the arrears are correct. Partially uphold the complaint and follow the Redress Calculation Policy.

## 2. Payment plans

Payment plans may last up to 12 months. Pause collections activity, including default notices, from the day the plan is agreed.

## 3. Default notices

Do not issue a default notice before three missed payments. Withdraw any notice sent while a payment plan is in place and uphold the complaint.
