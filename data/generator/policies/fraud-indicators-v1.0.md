# Fraud Indicators

Version 1.0. Effective from 1 July 2024. Restricted to team leads.

## 1. Account indicators

- Contact details changed shortly before a refund request.
- Redress requested to a bank account not held by the customer.
- Several accounts sharing a phone number or email address.

## 2. Action

Stop any payment and refer the case to the fraud team the same day. Do not tell the customer that fraud is suspected.
