# Arrears Handling Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 16 January 2023.

## 1. Contact limits

Contact a customer in arrears no more than five times in any seven days, across all channels.

## 2. Payment plans

Payment plans may last up to six months. Collections activity continues until the first plan payment is received.

## 3. Default notices

A default notice may be issued after two missed payments.
