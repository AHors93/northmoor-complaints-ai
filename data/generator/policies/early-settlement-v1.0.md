# Early Settlement Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 2 October 2023.

## 1. Right to settle

Customers may settle all or part of an agreement early. Provide a settlement figure on request.

## 2. Charge

An early settlement charge may apply. It must be set out in the pre-contract information.

## 3. Complaints

Do not uphold the complaint if the charge was set out in signed pre-contract information and the sales call explains it. Otherwise, refund the charge.
