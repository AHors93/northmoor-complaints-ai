# Affordability Checks Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 1 February 2024.

## 1. Before lending

Verify income using payslips, bank statements or a credit reference agency income check. Review existing debts and essential spending.

## 2. Evidence

Keep the evidence for six years after the agreement ends. If there is no evidence, treat the check as not done.

## 3. Complaints

If income was not verified, uphold the complaint and refund the interest charged up to the date the complaint was received.
