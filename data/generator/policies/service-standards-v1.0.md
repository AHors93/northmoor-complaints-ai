# Customer Service Standards

Version 1.0. Effective from 3 June 2024.

## 1. Telephone

Answer calls within ten minutes. If a call drops, ring the customer back the same day.

## 2. Conduct

Be polite and clear. Listen to the recording before deciding a complaint about conduct.

## 3. Complaints

If the query was resolved but the wait exceeded the standard, partially uphold the complaint. If the recording shows the adviser acted properly, do not uphold it.
