# Vulnerable Customer Procedures

Version 1.0. Effective from 5 August 2024. Restricted to team leads.

## 1. Identifying vulnerability

Consider health, life events, resilience and capability. Lost sleep, bereavement, illness or job loss should prompt a review.

## 2. Support

Offer breathing space of 60 days, a different contact channel or a named case owner.

## 3. Escalation

If there is any risk of harm, a team lead must contact the customer the same day.
