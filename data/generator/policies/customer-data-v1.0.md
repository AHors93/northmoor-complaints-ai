# Customer Identification and Data Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 15 April 2024.

## 1. Security checks

Do not discuss an account until the caller has passed security checks.

## 2. Change of address

Update the address within two working days of a verified request.

## 3. Misdirected post

If we failed to update an address and letters went astray, uphold the complaint and report the incident to the data protection team.
