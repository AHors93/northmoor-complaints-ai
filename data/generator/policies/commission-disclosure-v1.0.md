# Commission Disclosure Policy

Version 1.0. Effective from 2 September 2024.

## 1. Disclosure

The agreement must state that the dealer may receive commission. Advisers must explain its existence and nature in plain language.

## 2. Interest rates

Commission must never vary with the interest rate offered to the customer.

## 3. Complaints

If commission was disclosed in writing but not clearly explained, partially uphold the complaint. If it was not disclosed at all, uphold it and refer the case to a team lead.
