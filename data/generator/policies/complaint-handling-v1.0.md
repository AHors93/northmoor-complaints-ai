# Complaint Handling Procedure

Version 1.0. Effective from 8 January 2024.

## 1. Recording

Record every expression of dissatisfaction on the day it is received. Assign one of four categories: fees, arrears, mis-selling or service.

## 2. Time limits

Acknowledge promptly. Send a final response within eight weeks of receipt. If that is not possible, write to explain the delay and the right to refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## 3. Outcomes

- Upheld: we were at fault.
- Partially upheld: we were at fault in part, or a goodwill refund is due under policy.
- Not upheld: we acted correctly.

## 4. Final response

State the outcome, the reasons and any redress. Tell the customer they have six months to refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service. A named person must approve every final response.
